<h1 align="center">Hi, I'm Gabriel Melo</h1>
<h3 align="center">Database Researcher</h3>

**M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Computational Mathematics at the University of São Paulo (USP/ICMC)**, funded by **FAPESP**, and collaborative researcher at the **Institute of Advanced Studies (IEA-USP)**.

My research lies at the intersection of **Database Systems**, **Data Engineering**, and **Artificial Intelligence**. I investigate techniques for efficient data management, query optimization, indexing, and scalable infrastructures that support machine learning and generative AI applications. My recent work also explores optimization methods for data mining and clustering algorithms applied to high-dimensional and complex datasets.

This GitHub hosts the research software, experimental implementations, and reproducible artifacts accompanying my publications and ongoing research projects.

> **Current Research:** Database Systems | Query Optimization | Data Engineering | AI Infrastructure | Data Mining
---

## Research Interests

* **Database Systems:** Query optimization, indexing techniques, storage structures, and efficient processing for relational and NoSQL databases.
* **AI Data Infrastructure:** Vector databases, semantic retrieval, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and scalable data pipelines.
* **Data Mining & Machine Learning:** Optimization of clustering algorithms, high-dimensional data analysis, and evaluation methodologies.
* **Data Engineering:** Architectures for integrating, processing, and managing large-scale structured and unstructured data.
